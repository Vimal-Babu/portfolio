The Story Behind
the Code

I didn't take the traditional path into tech. After graduating with an Engineering degree, I spent years in sales, logistics, and community support work across Kerala and Bengaluru — including driving cabs on Ola and supporting palliative care nurses on home visits.

Those years taught me consistency, how to work under pressure, and how to show up for people. In 2023, I made a deliberate decision to transition into software development — and I haven't looked back.

I joined Brototype's intensive self-learning program, mastered Python and Django, and have since built full-stack applications from scratch — deployed on real cloud infrastructure.
